Our Name

Named after a bird that doesn't know it's small.

Etuen comes from Ituen, an Ibibio word from southeastern Nigeria for a hawk known for one trait above all: though smaller than the birds around it, it chases down the bigger ones anyway. We spell it Etuen for clearer pronunciation — the meaning stays exactly the same.

That's the whole idea behind this company. Small firms shouldn't have to wait until they're bigger to look, work, and compete like they belong at the top of their field. We build the digital footing that lets you fly like you already do.
